# The Summer Hikaru Died

*A horror-drama manga about friendship, identity, and loss.*

---

## Overview
**The Summer Hikaru Died** (*Hikaru ga Shinda Natsu*) is a manga by **Ren Mokumoku**.
It follows **Yoshiki**, who realizes that his best friend **Hikaru** has been replaced by something not human.

---

## Why I Like It
- emotional and haunting story
- beautiful artwork
- a mix of fear and deep friendship
